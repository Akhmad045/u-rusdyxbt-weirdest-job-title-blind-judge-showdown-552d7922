## The entry

> **Professional Mourner for Funerals of Complete Strangers**

(55 characters — one line, nothing else.)

## Why this title

The judge's brief rewards a real-sounding job with one hilariously specific
twist over a generic oddity. Professional mourners are a real job — people
have been hired to grieve at funerals for centuries — but being hired to
weep for someone you never met, at a stranger's funeral, is the twist that
lands on the first read: instantly pictureable, stranger than ordinary work,
and short enough to take in at a glance.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+, no dependencies, no network, no randomness) writes the
single job title to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
